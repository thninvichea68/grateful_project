Port

Port No, Port Code and Port Name come from the port list.

Invoice date

Invoice date is 1 day after the declare date.

Working days

Skip Saturday, Sunday and public holidays.

CM (commissions)

50 USD per shipment for every factory, except JR Apparel.

Net profit

[ INV (tax) + REIM + DN ]
− [ Clear Fee + THC + CM + Other Pay ]
